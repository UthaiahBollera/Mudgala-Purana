## Three unresolved readings

- [53:41, PT881](https://books.google.com/books?id=Lh0FEQAAQBAJ&pg=PT881): the print reads **योगतः**. The Kannada follows that reading, with a visible doctrinal-review caveat; no negative prefix is silently inserted.
- [54:10, PT889](https://books.google.com/books?id=Lh0FEQAAQBAJ&pg=PT889): **योगमानवान्** remains uncertain. The secure portion is translated and the omitted uncertain meaning is visibly identified.
- [54:53, PT902](https://books.google.com/books?id=Lh0FEQAAQBAJ&pg=PT902): **विकुण्ठं** remains lexically uncertain. It is not replaced by **वैकुण्ठं** or an invented destination.

These are accepted only as explicitly qualified renderings, not as resolved passages. They are indexed in [unresolved-passages.json](unresolved-passages.json), alongside prior issues and subsequent resolutions. Unknown locations remain null.

## Resolved drafting questions

Image inspection confirmed **साधनैः** at 54:2, **सुकथानकम्** at 54:34, **वै** in 54:15, and long **ई** in **दास्यामीप्सितं** at 54:26. At 54:29, **जनको** refers to the father in the singular. At 54:40, **भावयुक्ताच्च** is ordinary sandhi for **भावयुक्तात् + च**, agreeing with **श्रवणात्**; the Kannada renders devotional hearing without changing the printed text. The colophon begins **ओमिति**.
