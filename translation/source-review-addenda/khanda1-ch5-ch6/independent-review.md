### 1.5.40
Printed reading:
तत्रैव दृश्यते देवो योगिभिर्योगपारगैः । तेन स्वानंदवासी स कथ्यते वेदवादिभिः ॥४०॥

Before:
ದೇವತೆಗಳೂ ಯೋಗಿಗಳೂ ಅವನನ್ನೇ ಆರಾಧಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ. ಆದ್ದರಿಂದ ವೇದಗಳು ಅವನನ್ನು ಸ್ವಾನಂದವಾಸಿ ಎಂದು ಕರೆಯುತ್ತವೆ. ॥೪೦॥

Exact proposed full replacement:
ಯೋಗದಲ್ಲಿ ಪಾರಂಗತರಾದ ಯೋಗಿಗಳಿಗೆ ದೇವನು ಅಲ್ಲಿಯೇ ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾನೆ. ಆದ್ದರಿಂದ ವೇದವಾದಿಗಳು ಅವನನ್ನು ಸ್ವಾನಂದವಾಸಿ ಎಂದು ಕರೆಯುತ್ತಾರೆ. ॥೪೦॥

Actual print at line end is योगिभिर्योग- and the next line begins पारगैः. The visible yoga spelling is the basis of the correction, not expected doctrine. Current plural gods are unsupported: देवः is the singular subject of दृश्यते, with yogins as perceivers. Worship is not the printed verb. वेदवादिभिः refers to speakers/exponents of the Veda, not the Vedas themselves. The historical online याग reading differs from this original; retain that discrepancy as a transcription/source difference, not an established printed-edition variant.

### 1.6.8
Printed reading:
स्वत उत्थानके नैव विकल्पः सदृशः कृतः । अहमस्मीति भावेन बिंबं भिन्नं विनिःसृतम् ॥८॥

Before:
ಸ್ವತಃ ಉತ್ಥಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಂತಹ ವಿಕಲ್ಪವೇ ಉಂಟಾಗಲಿಲ್ಲ. ‘ನಾನಿದ್ದೇನೆ’ ಎಂಬ ಭಾವದಿಂದ ಪ್ರತ್ಯೇಕವಾದ ಬಿಂಬವೊಂದು ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮಿತು. ॥೮॥

Proposal: no new text replacement; retain unresolved flag.

नैव is clear. Current Kannada preserves the negation, I-am thought, distinct bimba and emergence. The force of स्वत उत्थानके and सदृशः remains compressed; no new minimal correction is demonstrated. Do not remove the negation, recast this as affirmative differentiation, or import a philosophical system. Not counted as fixed.

### 1.6.12
Printed reading:
मायया बिंबरूपोऽयं जातः सद्यश्च नामतः । माया बिंबं द्वितीयं तत्तत्र मोहप्रधारणात् ॥१२॥

Before:
ಮಾಯೆಯಿಂದ ವಿಶ್ವಸ್ವರೂಪವು ಹುಟ್ಟಿತು. ಅದರ ನಾನಾ ಮುಖಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಮೋಹವನ್ನು ಹೊತ್ತ ಬುದ್ಧಿಯು ಇನ್ನೊಂದು ಮಾಯೆಯಾಯಿತು. ॥೧೨॥

Exact proposed provisional partial text, with hold kept visible:
ಮಾಯೆಯಿಂದ ಇದು ತಕ್ಷಣವೇ ಬಿಂಬರೂಪವಾಯಿತು. ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಮೋಹವನ್ನು ಧರಿಸುವುದರಿಂದ ಮಾಯೆಯೇ ಎರಡನೆಯ ಬಿಂಬವಾಗಿದೆ. [‘ಚ ನಾಮತಃ’ ಎಂಬುದರ ಅನ್ವಯ ಮತ್ತು ಬಿಂಬಗಳ ನಿಖರ ಸಂಬಂಧ ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆಯಲ್ಲಿವೆ.] ॥೧೨॥

The current world-form, many faces and intellect are not stated in this verse. Bimba occurs twice; māyā, second, immediate arising and bearing delusion are explicit. Intellect is discussed in subsequent context but must not be imported into this verse as its subject. Candidate is a provisional literal account, not a complete doctrinal resolution: the attachment of ca nāmataḥ, the antecedent of ayam/tat and the precise relationship of the bimbas remain held. Full unqualified replacement is null.

### 1.6.13
Printed reading:
मोहधारणमात्रेण विस्मृतं स्वस्वरूपकम् । मायासुखेप्सुना जाता तस्य देवस्य तत्क्षणात् ॥१३॥

Before:
ಮೋಹವನ್ನು ಧರಿಸಿದ ಮಾತ್ರದಿಂದ ಸ್ವಸ್ವರೂಪವು ಮರೆತುಹೋಯಿತು. ಸುಖವನ್ನು ಬಯಸಿದ ಆ ದೇವನಿಗೆ ತಕ್ಷಣವೇ ಮಾಯೆಯು ಉಂಟಾಯಿತು. ॥೧೩॥

Proposal: no new text replacement; retain unresolved flag.

Printed मायासुखेप्सुना is continuous. The current Kannada follows māyā sukhepsunā and associates the desire for happiness with that deva. This remains a provisional construal: the instrumental सुखेप्सुना and its relation to जाता / तस्य देवस्य are not settled by typography. First half matches overt source. No new replacement is recommended and the verse is not counted as fixed.

### 1.6.20
Printed reading:
स एव नामतस्तत्र न गतो निजरूपधृक् । बिंबबिंबिप्रभावत्वाद्भासते तादृशो वृथा ॥२०॥

Before:
ತನ್ನ ನಿಜರೂಪವನ್ನು ಹೊಂದಿರುವ ಅವನು ಅಲ್ಲಿಗೆ ನಿಜವಾಗಿ ಬಂದವನಲ್ಲ; ಹೆಸರಿನಿಂದ ಮಾತ್ರ ಹಾಗೆ ಹೇಳಲ್ಪಡುತ್ತಾನೆ. ಬಿಂಬ ಮತ್ತು ಪ್ರತಿಬಿಂಬಗಳ ಪ್ರಭಾವದಿಂದ ಹಾಗೆ ತೋರುತ್ತಾನೆ. ॥೨೦॥

Proposal: no new text replacement; retain unresolved flag.

The negative न गतो is clear, as are नामतस्तत्र and the bimba-bimbi compound. Current Kannada preserves the negative and name-related clause, but its ನಿಜವಾಗಿ / ಮಾತ್ರ are interpretive, and ಪ್ರತಿಬಿಂಬ is a gloss for bimbi rather than the printed pratibimba. The force of वृथा (e.g. vainly/falsely) is not separately rendered. These remain interpretation/coverage holds; without resolving the compound and clause attachment, no unconditional replacement is recommended. Do not count it as fixed or certify the existing philosophical gloss.
