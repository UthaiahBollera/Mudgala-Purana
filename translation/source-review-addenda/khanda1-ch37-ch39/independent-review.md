### 1.37.39
Printed source:
मूढोऽसि येन संग्रामं कर्तुमागत एव च । न मे मृत्युर्भवेदष्टावरणैर्देहधारिभिः ॥३९॥

Before:
ಮೂಢನಾಗಿರುವುದರಿಂದಲೇ ನನ್ನೊಡನೆ ಯುದ್ಧಮಾಡಲು ಬಂದಿರುವೆ. ಎಂಟು ಆವರಣಗಳಿಂದಾಗಲಿ ದೇಹಧಾರಿಗಳಿಂದಾಗಲಿ ನನಗೆ ಮರಣವಿಲ್ಲ. ॥೩೯॥

After:unchanged. Replacement:null.

unchanged_grammatical_relationship_hold
Page84 prints the entire verse text; only its numeral continues on85. The eight enclosures and embodied-bearing instrumental forms are clear. Current Kannada coordinates them as two separate causes (ಆವರಣಗಳಿಂದಾಗಲಿ ದೇಹಧಾರಿಗಳಿಂದಾಗಲಿ). The Sanskrit can instead attach embodied-bearing as a qualifier to enclosures; surrounding37–41 does not settle the relation. Retain provisionally, without certifying that coordination or replacing it by a different unsupported construction. No weapons reading is supported.
