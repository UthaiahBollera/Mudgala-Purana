## Target findings

### 1.4.3
Printed reading (spacing regularized; anusvāra spellings retained):
नानाऽपशकुनं तत्र बभूवातिभयानकम् । स्तुतिभिस्तेन सर्वे ते उत्पाताशंकया भृशम् ॥३॥

Before:
ಬ್ರಹ್ಮನನ್ನೂ ಭಯಪಡಿಸುವ ಅನೇಕ ಅಪಶಕುನಗಳು ಉಂಟಾದವು. ವಿವಿಧ ಸ್ತುತಿಗಳಿಂದ ಅವರೆಲ್ಲರೂ ಶೂಲಪಾಣಿಯನ್ನು ಹೊಗಳುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು. ॥೩॥

Demonstrated defects: अतिभयानकम् means extremely frightening; Brahmā is absent. उत्पाताशंकया भृशम् supplies intense apprehension of portent/calamity, omitted by the current text. स्तुतिभिः is an instrumental, but neither a finite praising verb nor the name Śūlapāṇi occurs in this half. An inferred praise action may be possible in context; it must not be represented as fully explicit.

Exact proposed partial text (not an unqualified full resolution):
ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಅತ್ಯಂತ ಭಯಂಕರವಾದ ನಾನಾ ಅಪಶಕುನಗಳು ಉಂಟಾದವು.

Second half has stutibhiḥ and no explicit finite verb. Do not retain an unmarked supplied praise action or identify its object as Śūlapāṇi. First-sentence replacement is secure; full replacement remains withheld.

### 1.4.8
Printed reading (spacing regularized; anusvāra spellings retained):
न किंचिदुक्तवांस्तत्र शंभुक्रोधभयाकुलः । शोकाग्निर्मां दहेत्किं वा ततो देवोऽन्वबुध्यत ॥८॥

Before:
ನನಗೆ ಏನನ್ನೂ ತಿಳಿಸದೆ, ನನ್ನ ಶೋಕವನ್ನು ನಿವಾರಿಸದೆ ಹೋದಳೇ? ದೇವತೆಗಳ ಮಾತಿನಿಂದ ತನ್ನ ದೇಹವನ್ನು ಸುಟ್ಟಳೇ? ॥೮॥

Demonstrated defects: the printed first half describes someone saying nothing because of fear of Śambhu’s anger. The current questions about Satī leaving without informing, relieving grief, and burning her body because of gods’ words are absent. Verse 7 supplies the exchange with Nārada; verse 9 proceeds to Śiva’s grief. This context does not remove all ambiguity in the intervening question.

Exact proposed partial text (not an unqualified full resolution):
ಶಂಭುವಿನ ಕೋಪದ ಭಯದಿಂದ ವ್ಯಾಕುಲನಾದ ಅವನು ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಏನನ್ನೂ ಹೇಳಲಿಲ್ಲ. “ಶೋಕಾಗ್ನಿಯು ನನ್ನನ್ನು ಸುಟ್ಟೀತೋ?” ಬಳಿಕ ದೇವನು ಅರಿತುಕೊಂಡನು. [ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಯ ವಕ್ತೃ ಮತ್ತು ವಾಕ್ಯಗಳ ಅನ್ವಯ ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆಯಲ್ಲಿವೆ.] ॥೮॥

The first subject is contextually Nārada but not named in this verse; the candidate uses a pronoun. The speaker/attachment of māṃ dahet kiṃ vā and the object of anvabudhyata remain unresolved. The question is preserved without attributing it to Satī or converting it to a claim about her body.

### 1.4.15
Printed reading (spacing regularized; anusvāra spellings retained):
इति गत्वा स्वयं शंभुर्मनसा गणपं भजन् । भक्त्यैकचेतसा चैव ध्यात्वा रूपमनुत्तमम् ॥१५॥

Before:
ಹೀಗೆ ಮನಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಗಣಪತಿಯನ್ನು ಭಜಿಸುತ್ತಾ ಶಂಭುವು ಭಕ್ತಿಯಿಂದ ಏಕಾಗ್ರಚಿತ್ತನಾಗಿ ಅವನ ಅನುಪಮ ರೂಪವನ್ನು ಧ್ಯಾನಿಸಿದನು. ॥೧೫॥

Proposal: retain the current Kannada; no new replacement.

Current text already removes the historical breath-control and one-syllable additions. Retain iti gatvā as an unresolved transition/movement expression. Do not invent a destination or silently emend gatvā to jñātvā. Dhyātvā is an absolutive in the extended meditation sequence; current finite Kannada is narrative recasting, not evidence of a Sanskrit finite verb.
