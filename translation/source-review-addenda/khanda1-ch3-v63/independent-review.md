## Clear Kannada defects

Current:
ಮುನಿಗಳೇ, ಬಳಿಕ ಅವಳು ಬಾಲ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ತಪಸ್ಸನ್ನು ಮಾಡಿ, ಸಂತೋಷವನ್ನು ಪಡೆದಳು. ಪಾರ್ವತಿಯ ಪ್ರೇಮದಿಂದ ಶಿವನೂ ಆನಂದಭರಿತನಾದನು. ॥೬೩॥

- No vocative addressing sages appears in63; remove ಮುನಿಗಳೇ.
- No childhood expression appears; remove ಬಾಲ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ. Verse62's birth as Himalaya's daughter does not supply childhood in63.
- सुतीव्रं explicitly qualifies the intense austerity; restore its force.
- पार्वती and प्रेमविह्वला are feminine nominatives, as is सा. Parvati is the subject of लेभे ('obtained'). शिवेन is instrumental, not the subject who becomes delighted. The current second sentence reverses the subject/affect attribution.
- The clear reading supports Parvati, overwhelmed with love, obtaining joy with Shiva. It does not independently say Shiva became delighted because of her love.

## Meaning hold

आत्मवशेन may attach differently than the proposed 'self-restraint' reading; the instrumental शिवेन permits another attachment involving Shiva. I do not adjudicate that construction here. Neither an asserted self-discipline reading nor 'Shiva under her control' is established by this review. The context59–62 supports the narrative sequence and Parvati's identity but does not settle this attachment.
