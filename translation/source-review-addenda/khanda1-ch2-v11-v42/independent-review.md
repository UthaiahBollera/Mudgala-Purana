## Verse 11: partial correction supported; final meaning hold remains

Original page0004 reads (spaces/dandas regularized):
एतत्सर्वं सुविस्तार्य पृच्छते ब्रूहि सादरम् ।
ज्ञात्वा तं देवदेवेशं भविष्यामि हितावहम् ॥११॥

Google PT84 differs in the first pada: एतत्सर्वं सविस्तारं. It agrees on the problematic final clause भविष्यामि हितावहम्. Preserve the original सुविस्तार्य; do not harmonize it to the reference.

The first half asks for an explanation in detail, respectfully, to the one asking (the first-person petitioner is supplied by context). Current 'ನನ್ನ ಹಿತಕ್ಕಾಗಿ' is not an exact rendering of this half; ಸಾದರಮ್ is also missing. Neither source says 'ಭಕ್ತನಾಗುವೆನು'. That devotional conclusion is unsupported. However, the printed final construction is grammatically awkward; do not silently emend भविष्यामि or हितावहम्, or accept a guessed beneficial/devotional outcome.

Exact supported first-sentence replacement:
ಕೇಳುತ್ತಿರುವ ನನಗೆ ಇವೆಲ್ಲವನ್ನು ವಿವರವಾಗಿ ಆದರದಿಂದ ಹೇಳು.

No unqualified full-verse replacement is recommended. If a visibly partial draft is needed, use:
ಕೇಳುತ್ತಿರುವ ನನಗೆ ಇವೆಲ್ಲವನ್ನು ವಿವರವಾಗಿ ಆದರದಿಂದ ಹೇಳು. ಆ ದೇವದೇವೇಶನನ್ನು ತಿಳಿದು ನಾನು… [‘ಭವಿಷ್ಯಾಮಿ ಹಿತಾವಹಮ್’ ಎಂಬ ಭಾಗದ ನಿಖರ ಅರ್ಥ ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆಯಲ್ಲಿದೆ.]” ॥೧೧॥

This is an explicitly incomplete translation, not a resolved final text. Existing 'ನಾನು ಅವನ ಭಕ್ತನಾಗುವೆನು' must not be treated as source-supported while the hold remains. Verse12's transition to the sages listening to Suta need not be rewritten from these crops; it belongs to the surrounding narration.

## Verse 42: demonstrable translation corrections

Both original page0005 and Google PT92 visibly read:
कलौ प्राप्ते च पाखण्डा भविष्यन्ति जटाधराः ।
शूद्रा नेदं पठिष्यन्ति नग्ना भस्माङ्गलेपनाः ॥४२॥

नेदं is clear in both sources. Do not replace it with वेदं. A literal न + इदं reading yields 'will not read/recite this'; the demonstrative's referent is not explicitly resolved here, so retain 'ಇದನ್ನು', not 'ವೇದವನ್ನು'. नग्ना is also clear. Current Kannada omits nakedness and reading/recitation and adds an unsupported comparison ('ಶೂದ್ರರಂತೆ') and falling ('ಪತನಗೊಳ್ಳುವರು'). Falling into hell belongs to the following verse43, not this verse42.

Proposed replacement, preserving the hostile speech as part of the narrative rather than endorsing it:
ಕಲಿಯುಗವು ಬಂದಾಗ ಜಟಾಧಾರಿಗಳು ಪಾಷಂಡಿಗಳಾಗುವರು. ಅವರು ಶೂದ್ರರೂ ನಗ್ನರೂ ಅಂಗಗಳಿಗೆ ಭಸ್ಮವನ್ನು ಹಚ್ಚಿಕೊಂಡವರೂ ಆಗುವರು; ಇದನ್ನು ಪಠಿಸುವುದಿಲ್ಲ. ॥೪೨॥

The linkage of the descriptors follows the continuing plural subject; retain a note that 'ಇದನ್ನು' leaves the referent unspecified. Do not turn this into general advice about caste or a claim about modern religious groups. No Sanskrit emendation is proposed.
