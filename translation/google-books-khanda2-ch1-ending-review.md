# Khanda 2 chapter 1 ending: verses 51–55

The five verses and colophon were independently reviewed against original Sanskrit at Google Books fZcHEQAAQBAJ: PT81 (51), PT82 (52–54), and PT83 (55 and colophon). All five translations are accepted. The colophon confirms the chapter ends at55 and names **ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಸೃಷ್ಟಿಯ ಆರಂಭ**, the beginning of Brahma’s work of creation.

Verse53 explicitly describes offering Siddhi and Buddhi at the occasion of dakshina. No marriage, daughterhood or wife relationship has been inserted. Verse54 names Brahma as the worshipper for clarity. Verse55 describes his intention to create, not the completed creation.

The colophon’s opening is **ओमिति**, correcting draft ॐमिति. Its tentative parenthesized cumulative count **२८५१** remains an open metadata question, excluded from the Kannada reading text. No missing verses or count reconciliation is inferred. Exact source links and decisions are retained in JSON.
