# Google Books review: Khanda 2 chapter 1, verses 26–50

All 25 records were read against eight original Sanskrit crops at Google Books fZcHEQAAQBAJ, PT74–PT81, and independently reviewed. Twenty-four were accepted directly; the narrower Kannada wording ಮಹಾಜಲರಾಶಿಯಲ್ಲಿ for महाजले was applied at verse29. No new unresolved meaning remains after review.

Verses32–39 are Brahma’s hymn and retain Sanskrit in Kannada script. The surrounding17 verses are formal Kannada narrative. Speaker context is established by verses31 and43. Chapter1 remains partial through verse50 and has no closing colophon.

## Source-reading questions resolved

- [28, PT75](https://books.google.com/books?id=fZcHEQAAQBAJ&pg=PT75): the dense conjunct सञ्छादितं was confirmed; the meaning is clothed/covered.
- [34, PT76](https://books.google.com/books?id=fZcHEQAAQBAJ&pg=PT76): enlargement confirms वै, not the draft transcription ते. Kannada transliteration uses ವೈ. The Sanskrit explicitly retains thumb-joint size, अङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं.
- [46, PT80](https://books.google.com/books?id=fZcHEQAAQBAJ&pg=PT80): printed तत्राऽदौ includes the avagraha omitted in the initial draft. Meaning is unchanged.
- [49, PT81](https://books.google.com/books?id=fZcHEQAAQBAJ&pg=PT81): enlarged pixels show मदीयेऽद्य and मेऽधुनाज्ञया, correcting the initial draft मदीयोऽद्य and मेऽधुना यया. The distinction was checked against nearby glyphs by independent readings. This is a transcription correction, not an emendation of the printed edition. The verse directs creation, by Ganesh’s command, of a universe like the moving-and-unmoving world seen in his belly.
